# Slice 57: Plenix's source in TypeScript

- **Issues:** #57 (owner direction), #61 (`any` types). **ADR:** 0011 (D-0045).
- **What the owner gets:** every Plenix part is written in TypeScript, and apps get exact types (no `any`), generated by the TypeScript compiler, not by reading comments.

## How big it is (measured 2026-11-05)

- About 244 files and 37,700 lines are still JavaScript: 50 of 146 component folders, 96 of 99 pattern folders, all 39 chart files, 6 templates, and 5 small files elsewhere.
- 383 of 2,736 published props (14%) are typed `any` today.

## Risks

- **Stricter types can break an app that passed wrong values.** Each wave's changelog lists the changed prop types under *Changed*. CTM builds against the new types before a release.
- **DataGrid (41 files) is the biggest.** It gets its own PRs, after the smaller components.
- **Review load.** Waves are serial; one wave is open at a time.
