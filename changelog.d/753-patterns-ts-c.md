### Changed

- TypeScript wave 3, batch C (#753, ADR 0011): twelve patterns are now `.tsx` with exported prop types: `QueryBuilder` (`QueryBuilderProps`), `QuickFilter` (`QuickFilterProps`, `QuickFilterOption`, `QuickFilterDate`), `Recommendation` (`RecommendationProps`, `RecommendationItem`), `RecordPage` and `RecordPage.Header` (`RecordPageProps`, `RecordPageHeaderProps`), `RelatedAssets` (`RelatedAssetsProps`, `RelatedAsset`), `RenditionList` (`RenditionListProps`, `Rendition`), `ResourceList` (`ResourceListProps`, `ResourceItem`), `Retry` (`useRetry` is generic with `RetryState` and `RetryOptions`; `RetryNoticeProps`), `ReviewFlow`, `ReviewQueue` and `WorkflowStatus` (`ReviewFlowProps`, `ReviewQueueProps`, `WorkflowStatusProps`, `WorkflowStatusName`, `ReviewDecision`), `RoleManager` (`RoleManagerProps`, `Role`, `RolePermission`, `RoleChange`), `SaveAs` (`SaveAsProps`) and `SearchWorkspace` (`SearchWorkspaceProps`, `SearchFilter`, `SearchChoice`). No `any` in a public prop. No behavior change.
