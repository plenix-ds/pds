### Fixed
- Field: in a Stack of inline fields, a label tip is again at most `--plx-comp-field-grid-label-tip-max-width` and may shrink, so a long label ends in an ellipsis as designed; Tooltip's own size rule had won over it. Field now passes the sizes through Tooltip's context tokens (#1337).
- Field: a cut label's full text now also shows while focus is anywhere in its Field (its control, the (i) button), so keyboard users can read it; the label is not a tab stop, and Escape closes the tip (#1337).
- Field: the label's text sits in its own span (`plx-field__label-text`) that isolates its direction, so a long English label in an Arabic page, or an Arabic one in an English page, is cut at its end; the required mark stays drawn when the label is cut (#1337).

### Deprecated
- `--plx-comp-file-browser-body-gap`: nothing reads it. The body's gap is `--plx-comp-file-browser-tree-gap` (it shows only between the tree and the content). It keeps working and is removed in 2.0.0 (#1337, D-0177).
