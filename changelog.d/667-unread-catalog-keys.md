### Removed

- 10 built-in catalog words that no Plenix part read (#667, D-0085): `dataGrid.showAll`, `dataGrid.narrower`, `dataGrid.moveLeft`, `dataGrid.moveRight`, `dataGrid.ungroup`, `providers.theme-boot.compact`, `providers.theme-boot.standard`, `providers.theme-boot.comfortable`, `utils.clipboard.failed` and `commerce.orders.notRefundable`. They are removed from English (`en.json`, `en.js`) and Arabic (`ar.js`). Nothing on screen changes. A search of the apps found no reader: CTM 9fa6386 has none of them (`notRefundable` in `static/ctm/applicant-filing.js` is CTM's own dictionary), and the St. John site c12c3e0 has none of them (it only loads `theme-boot.js`). An app that passes its own words through a part's `strings` prop, or through `createTranslator`, is not affected.
