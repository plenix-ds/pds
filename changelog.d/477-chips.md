### Added

- Combobox `chipOverflow`: `"wrap"` (default, as before) or `"count"`, which keeps a multiple Combobox's chosen chips on one line and folds the ones that do not fit into one "+N" Tag named "{count} more" (new words key `combobox.moreChips`, English; the Arabic needs the owner's approval). The DataGrid tags editor uses it (#477, ADR 0016 addendum, D-0065).
