### Changed

- **Review parts:** `WorkflowStatus` and `WORKFLOW_STATUSES` are their own module, and so are `ReviewFlow` and `ReviewQueue`, so an import of the status badge no longer loads the review panel and the queue's grid, command bar and prompt dialog. On top of the shared layers, `WorkflowStatus` adds 2.6 KB (was 7.7 KB), `ReviewQueue` 4.1 KB, `ReviewFlow` 7.1 KB and `ApprovalWorkflow` 9.6 KB (was 10.2 KB), and `Publish`, which only draws the badge, 4.3 KB (was 9.4 KB). The exports, props and published types are unchanged (ADR 0052). `MetricCard` is unchanged: it adds 7.7 KB on the layers, and its chart code is not what loads the charts layer.
