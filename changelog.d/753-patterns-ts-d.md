### Changed

- TypeScript wave 3, batch D (#753, ADR 0011): eleven patterns are now `.tsx` with exported prop types: `SecondaryPane` (`SecondaryPaneProps`), `SessionTimeout` (`SessionTimeoutProps`; `useSessionTimeout` takes `SessionTimeoutOptions` and returns `SessionTimeoutState`), `SettingsLayout`, `SettingsSection` and `SettingsRow` (`SettingsLayoutProps`, `SettingsSectionProps`, `SettingsRowProps`), `ShareDialog` (`ShareDialogProps`, `ShareGrant`), `ShortcutsDialog` (`ShortcutsDialogProps`), `SidePanel` (`SidePanelProps`), `SortPanel` (`SortPanelProps`, `SortLevel`), `SortableList` (`SortableListProps`, generic over the item), `SplitView` and `ReadingPane` (`SplitViewProps`, `ReadingPaneProps`), `StatusBar` (`StatusBarProps`) and `TimeGrid` (`TimeGridProps`, `TimeGridDay`, `TimeGridEvent`). No `any` in a public prop. No behavior change.
