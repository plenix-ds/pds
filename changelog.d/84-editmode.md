### Added

- DataGrid (ADR 0016, #84 F1): the **Editing** choice in the view menu. With `editModes={['inline', 'form']}` and an editable column, the view menu ends with a divider and an "Editing" group of two radio items, "Inline Edit" and "Form Edit"; the choice is reported through `onViewChange({ editMode })` and remembered per table under `storageKey`. `editMode` sets how a table opens when nothing is remembered. New `TableSettings` provider: application-wide `editMode`, `editModes` and `editing` defaults for the tables under it; a table's own props win over it, and the person's remembered choice wins over both. With one way offered (the default is `['inline']`) nothing appears and tables do not change. The choice has no effect on editing until Form Edit itself ships (F2).
