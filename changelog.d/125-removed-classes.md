### Removed

- 321 utility classes nothing uses, deprecated since earlier releases and in 0.90.0 (#125, VERSIONING.md): `plx-shadow-sm`, `-md`, `-lg`, `-raised`, `-menu`, `-modal`, `-ring`, `-ring-subtle`, `-underline-focus`; the `plx-span-*` classes that were not used (`plx-span-1`, `-4`, `-12` and `-16` stay); `plx-ratio-3-2`, `-3-4`, `-9-16`; `plx-control-toolbar`, `-standard`, `-dialog`; `plx-ai__mark`; and the `plx-u-*` utilities (the `fs`, `fw`, `leading` and `tracking` families, the one-off layout, background, padding, width and icon-box classes, and the rest of the list in `platform/standards/deprecated-classes.json` before this change). Each family's replacement is in the 0.90.0 *Deprecated* entry (a token on your own class, the Text component's variants, the layout parts). The going-away list is empty. The system stylesheets are 10.7 KB smaller; every element's computed style on the application pages is identical before and after.
