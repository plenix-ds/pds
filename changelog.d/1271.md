### Added

- **SettingsLayout:** `navTitle` (default "Settings"), `pane` (the nav pane's SecondaryPane props) and `strings`. New string `settingsLayout.navTitle`.
- **PanelSplitter:** `onReset`, the edge's Enter and double-click: back to the standard width.

### Changed

- **SettingsLayout:** the settings nav is a docked SecondaryPane (flush beside the main nav, full height, titled, collapsible, resizable, remembered), not a card in the content. At Narrow it is a list-detail list. `plx-settings__nav` and `plx-settings__frame` are no longer drawn (ADR 0057).
- **Navigation panes:** one width at every window size, 240px (the main nav and the secondary panes). They no longer widen at 1440px and 1920px. The secondary pane `md` was 220px; the nav minimum was 140px and is 200px; the edge moves 8px, not 16px. The side panel keeps its steps in an unframed shell.
- **Resize edge:** Enter or a double-click returns the nav and secondary panes to their standard width instead of folding them. Collapse and Ctrl+[ still fold.

### Deprecated

- The window-step tokens `shell-nav-width-from-wide`, `shell-nav-width-from-ultrawide` and `secondary-pane-width-{sm,md,lg}-from-wide` and `-from-ultrawide` are no longer read. They keep working and are removed in 2.0.0.

### Removed

- The unread tokens `settings-nav-border-bottom-color` and `settings-nav-border-bottom-width`.
