### Added

- DataGrid: with `editing.fill` and a selected block, the right-click menu has "Fill down" and "Fill right" (toward the inline end): a single-pointer alternative to dragging the fill handle (#654, WCAG 2.5.7). With a row menu the fill items come first. Without a block the browser's own menu is left alone. New words: Fill down, Fill right, Fill (the menu's name); Arabic to follow from the i18n Engineer.
